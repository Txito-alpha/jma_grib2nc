description=Convert JMA run-length packed GRIB2 (Radar/Raingauge-Analyzed Precipitation, nowcasts, etc.) to CF NetCDF without extra libraries.
about=Reads Japan Meteorological Agency GRIB2 files that use run-length packing (data representation template 5.200) - e.g. Radar/Raingauge-Analyzed Precipitation (解析雨量), Very-short-range Forecasting of Precipitation (降水短時間予報) and Precipitation Nowcasts - which GDAL cannot decode, and writes a CF-1.8 NetCDF-4 file with a time dimension. Pure Python decoder; requires only numpy and the GDAL bundled with QGIS (no wgrib2 / ecCodes needed). The output can be limited to an extent (map canvas, layer, drawn) or to the extent of (selected) features. The result is added as a time-aware mesh layer (Temporal Controller) or as a raster layer. Available as a Processing algorithm (batch capable) and from the Raster menu. 気象庁のランレングス圧縮GRIB2をNetCDFに変換します。
